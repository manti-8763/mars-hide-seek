MISSION BRIEFING / Mission briefing

The crew’s signal has been lost.

On Mars, terrain can block signals between the base and the crew, making it difficult to monitor the mission or send observations.

You are the mission communications officer. Find a relay location that connects the base to the astronaut’s last known position. You have one relay.

How do you solve it?

  1. Explore the 3D terrain or map to find a relay site.
  2. Compare terrain profiles and connection information for the base and destination.
  3. Place the relay and check the result. If the link fails, try another location.

Use the terrain and available information to make your own decision.

Why does antenna height matter?

Antenna height is measured above the ground. In this mission, the base is 4 m, the relay 3 m, and the destination 2 m. Greater height can improve visibility, but does not guarantee a connection.

The terrain is based on real data; characters and mission situations are fictional. Results do not guarantee real-world performance.
